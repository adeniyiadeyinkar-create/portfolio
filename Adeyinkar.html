About / Background

Turning Ideas Into Experiences

I'm Adeniyi Adeyinka, a multi-disciplinary creative who lives at the intersection of design, motion, and storytelling. From brand identities to cinematic 3D sequences, I bring a holistic perspective to every project.

My work spans graphic and product design, motion graphics, video editing, and immersive 3D worlds — always anchored in a deep understanding of the story being told and the audience feeling it.
